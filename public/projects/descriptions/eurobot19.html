Eurobot is an international robotics competition organized by Planète Sciences and the finals are played at Roche-sur-Yon in France and as the AeRobotix INSAT team of 2019, we won the 7th place award in these finals among 200 participating teams from all over the world.

Every participating team is tasked to create 2 robots that does a very specified list of tasks that are explained in the specification proposed by Planète Sciences. In the 2019 edition the theme was Atom Factory, so the robot was tasked to collect different types of atoms, sort them by color and put them in specified locations. The robots are fully autonomous and are required to navigate to do their tasks while avoiding the adversary robots and keep update on each other’s progress.

This competition was an opportunity for me to learn different things:
· Embedded_programing with STM32: TIMERs, Communication Protocols (UART, I2C, SPI) …
· Speed and position regulation
· Autonomous_navigation and obstacle avoidance
· Mechanical and Electrical design (PCB Design)
· RF communication

This was a great experience and thanks to my teammates and a whole year of hard work and persistence, we achieved great results.
